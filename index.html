Questions? Answered.

How do you help me get more reviews?

It depends on how your business works. Walk-in businesses (restaurants, cafés, shops, gyms) get a QR code customers can scan on the spot. Appointment-based businesses send a one-click review request by email or WhatsApp after the visit. Every customer lands on your Google review page — we never filter out unhappy customers, so your profile stays fully compliant with Google's policies.

How do review requests reach my customers?

Emails send automatically and are unlimited on every plan. Phone requests are sent by WhatsApp from the business's own number with one tap — free, unlimited, and customers reply to a number they already know and trust. Every request carries a personal link, so you always see who responded and how.

How does the AI draft review replies?

LocalPulseHub reads each incoming Google review and uses a large-language model to draft a personalized, on-brand reply in your chosen language and tone. Approve each reply yourself, or let auto-reply post it after a delay you choose.

Can I approve replies before they are published?

Yes. By default every reply is a draft until you approve it, by one-click email link or in the dashboard. You can also turn on auto-reply: replies post automatically after a delay you choose (for example 5 or 10 minutes), so you still have time to edit or cancel.

Which languages are supported?

We currently support English, German, French, and Spanish. You can also set formality preferences such as Sie/Du in German or Vous/Tu in French.

Is a credit card required for the free trial?

For the Founding offer on Pro, yes: you add your card at signup, but you're not charged for the first 2 months and can cancel anytime before. The Agency and Custom plans have a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Do you support multiple Google Business locations?

Yes. The Pro plan covers a single location and the Agency plan supports multiple locations, making it ideal for franchises and marketing agencies.

How do 1-star review alerts work?

When a new 1-star review is detected, LocalPulseHub sends an email alert within the hour so you can respond quickly.
